# Customer Support Playbook & Escalation Procedures

1. Triage customer queries based on urgency (billing disputes > defective items > general inquiries).
2. For return requests, check delivery timestamp. If within the standard policy window (15 days for Shopee/30 days for LazMall), provide the digital return label immediately.
3. If the item is defective, ask the buyer for clear pictures or an unboxing video showing the shipping label and defect.
4. Escalate unresolvable seller disputes to the Tier-2 Resolution Team within 24 hours.
